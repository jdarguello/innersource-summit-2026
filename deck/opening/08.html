Oscar · The opportunityShared foundation does not mean identical companies.

Common base, local choices and deliberate upgrades.

0:55 · Oscar · Opening 8/11

A shared foundation does not require one environment or identical configurations. [Step 1] These companies consume the same version of the common base. The numbers are examples. [Step 2] Each adds its own configuration and extensions: domains, infrastructure identifiers, local services and other contextual needs. [Step 3] A local change does not have to become a change to the common base. Conversely, a useful common improvement can return upstream. Each company decides when to adopt a new platform version. The design challenge is making that boundary explicit, so customization does not turn into disconnected copies. We want autonomy at the edges and shared learning in the foundation.
