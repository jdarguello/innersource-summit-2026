Oscar · The opportunityShared work creates familiar ground.

Less rediscovery when people collaborate across teams.

0:35 · Oscar · Opening 3/11

Sharing an artifact also shares decisions. The documentation explains why an interface works that way; the examples show how to use it. [Step 1] Now imagine an engineer moving from one team to another, or simply helping another team deliver. [Step 2] Familiar interfaces, examples and conventions give that person something they already recognize. This can reduce rediscovery and make collaboration easier. We want to preserve that advantage as the things teams share become larger.
