What if the reusable artifact were the whole foundation?

0:50 · Oscar · Opening 5/11

Even when teams share excellent components, they can still repeat the composition work. [Step 1] One company connects the pieces, configures their interfaces and decides how to operate the whole. [Step 2] Another assembles a similar platform in a different way. [Step 3] A third repeats the process. Versions, integrations and controls can diverge over time. That means an improvement may need to be rediscovered or reimplemented in several places. We are describing a risk to design against, not claiming these specific incidents occurred. The gap is between sharing the parts and sharing how the parts work together. That composition is itself work, and it can become a reusable artifact.
